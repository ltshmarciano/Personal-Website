Enchanted Kingdom

Enchanted Kingdom, located in Santa Rosa, Laguna, is the Philippines' first and most famous theme park, offering a world of excitement and adventure. Spanning over 25 hectares, the park is designed to transport visitors into a magical realm with themed zones inspired by fairy tales, space exploration, and cultural wonders. It features a variety of rides, from heart-pounding roller coasters like the Space Shuttle to refreshing water attractions like the Rio Grande Rapids.

I have never been to Enchanted Kingdom, but I’ve been to Star City. However, nothing beats the best amusement park in the Philippines! I want to experience the exciting rides they offer, from thrilling roller coasters to attractions that test my limits. I’m curious to see how dizzying and exhausting the rides can be by the end of the day, and I can’t wait to feel the rush of their scariest roller coasters and so much more.

How to get to there?

  • By Bus/Van:
    1. Ride a bus or van to Balibago, Sta. Rosa from terminals like Buendia, Lawton, or Cubao
    2. Get off at Waltermart Santa Rosa and take a tricycle or jeepney to Enchanted Kingdom
  • By P2P Bus (Direct Route):
    1. Some P2P buses from Ayala Malls South Park, Alabang go directly to Enchanted Kingdom
  • By Private Car:
    1. Take SLEX and exit at Santa Rosa (Exit 38)
    2. Follow the signs leading to Enchanted Kingdom.
