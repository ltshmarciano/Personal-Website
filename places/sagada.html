Sagada

Sagada is a peaceful mountain town in the Philippines known for its breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural heritage. It is famous for its hanging coffins, limestone caves, and scenic rice terraces. Visitors go there to enjoy cool weather, adventure activities, and a glimpse of traditional Igorot life.

I have visited Baguio many times, but I have never been to Sagada. I want to go there to experience its peaceful environment and natural beauty. I believe Sagada is a place where one can reflect, connect with oneself, and appreciate the deeper meaning of life.

How to get to there?

  • By Bus:
    1. Direct Route: Take a Coda Lines bus from Cubao directly to Sagada. Travel time is 10-12 hours, and fare ranges from ₱1,000 to ₱1,200.
    2. Via Baguio: Take a Victory Liner, Genesis, or JoyBus to Baguio (4-6 hours). From Baguio, ride a GL Trans or Lizardo Bus to Sagada (5-6 hours).
    3. Via Banaue and Bontoc: Take an Ohayami or Coda Lines bus to Banaue (9 hours). From Banaue, ride a van or bus to Bontoc (2 hours), then a jeepney to Sagada (1 hour).
  • By Plane:
    1. Fly from Manila to Cauayan Airport (Isabela) or Loakan Airport (Baguio) via Cebu Pacific or Philippine Airlines.
    2. From Cauayan, take a van to Bontoc (6-7 hours) and then a jeepney to Sagada (1 hour).
    3. From Baguio, take a bus or van to Sagada (5-6 hours).
  • By Private Car:
    1. Drive from Manila via NLEX – SCTEX – TPLEX, then pass through Baguio or Banaue towards Sagada.
    2. The trip takes around 10-12 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions.
    3. Roads can be steep and winding, so experienced drivers are recommended.
