Namsan Tower

Namsan Tower, also known as N Seoul Tower, is a famous landmark in Seoul, South Korea. It stands on Namsan Mountain and offers a stunning panoramic view of the city. Visitors can ride a cable car or hike up to the tower for a scenic experience. The tower is also known for its love locks, where couples attach padlocks as a symbol of their love.

South Korea is one of the countries my father frequently visits for work, as the main headquarters of his company is located there. During his trips, he often captures photos of Namsan Tower and shares them with us. I have also seen this iconic landmark featured in several K-dramas, and I am captivated by its mesmerizing beauty. I would love to visit the tower myself, place a padlock as a symbol of my wishes, and experience the breathtaking view from the observation deck.

How to get to there?

  • By Plane:
    1. Book a direct flight from Manila (NAIA), Cebu (MCIA), CRK or other major airports in the Philippines to ICN in Seoul.
    2. Take the Airport Railroad Express (AREX) to Seoul Station
    3. Once you arrive in Seoul, you have several options
  • By Namsan Cable Car (From Seoul Station):
    1. Take the subway to Myeongdong Station (Line 4, Exit 3).
    2. Walk 5–10 minutes to the Namsan Cable Car Station.
    3. Ride the cable car to the top, then walk to the tower entrance.
  • By Namsan Shuttle Bus (From Seoul Station):
    1. Take bus numbers 02, 03, or 05 from various points in Seoul, such as Seoul Station, Myeongdong, or Dongguk University.
    2. The bus will take you directly to Namsan Tower.
  • By Taxi (From Seoul Station):
    1. A taxi ride from central Seoul (e.g., Myeongdong or Seoul Station) takes about 10–15 minutes.
