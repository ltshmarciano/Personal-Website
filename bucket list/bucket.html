Travel Bucket List

  • Bucket List 1: Travel to 30 different countries
    • I want to visit 30 different countries before my 30th birthday. Traveling has always been a dream of mine, but I’ve never had the chance to go abroad. Exploring new cultures, tasting different cuisines, and meeting people from around the world excites me. This goal will push me to step out of my comfort zone and experience life beyond what I know. I believe it’s the perfect challenge because it will motivate me to work hard and make my dream a reality.
  • Bucket List 2: Live in Singapore for 3 years
    • After I graduate college, I want to move out of the Philippines to experience life in a different country. Singapore is my top choice because it is a global hub for technology and innovation. With its thriving IT industry, I believe it’s the perfect place for me to grow professionally and build a strong career. Living there for three years will allow me to adapt to a new culture, gain valuable work experience, and expand my opportunities. I see this as a major milestone in my journey toward success and personal growth.

Personal Growth Bucket List

  • Personal Growth Goal 1: Get BSCpE Degree
    • Earning a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BSCpE) is one of my biggest academic goals. I plan to pursue it right after high school because I am eager to dive into the tech industry. The field excites me with its mix of hardware, software, and problem-solving. I know it will be challenging, but I am willing to put in the effort to succeed. This degree will open many opportunities for me to innovate and grow in my future career.
  • Personal Growth Goal 2: Learn how to meditate
    • I have always been curious about meditation, but I don’t know how to do it properly. It seems like a great way to find inner peace and reduce stress. I want to learn it seriously because it looks calming and helps people stay focused. Through meditation, I hope to connect with myself on a deeper level and improve my mental well-being. Mastering this practice will allow me to stay balanced and mindful in my daily life.

Career & Finance Bucket List

  • Career & Goal 1: Be Financially Stable
    • Achieving financial stability is one of my top priorities in life. I don’t just want to earn enough to get by. I want to reach a point where I have an enormous amount of extra money, allowing me to live life on my own terms. Financial stability, for me, means having the freedom to make choices without constantly worrying about expenses. It means being able to travel, invest in my passions, support my loved ones, and take risks without fear of financial setbacks.
  • Career & Goal 2: Build a Business
    • I want to establish a stable and profitable business. One of my initial plans is to invest in rental apartments and share the profits with my parents. Additionally, I aspire to launch a startup company that focuses on solving challenges within the IT industry. This will allow me to combine my passion for technology with entrepreneurship. Building a successful business will not only secure my future but also create opportunities for others.
